# Photo and video credits

Real photographs and film from Antarctica, South Georgia and the Drake Passage, from Wikimedia Commons.
They are credited on the footer-linked /photo-credits page (generated from `data/photo-credits.json` and
`data/photo-credits.ts`), never on or under the photos. Photos in this folder are cropped to the layout's
aspect ratio where noted on the credits page and resized to about 1600 px, with an 800 px copy (`-800.jpg`).
The home hero (`../hero-background*.webp`) is the Lemaire Channel photo resized; `../hero-foreground*.webp`
is the same photo with the sky cut away. The two video loops (`../bottom-of-the-world.mp4`,
`../expedition-loop.mp4`) are short excerpts of one CC BY 3.0 film, re-encoded (the second cropped to portrait);
that file came to Commons from YouTube and is still awaiting Commons licence review.
CC BY-SA files are shared under the same licence.
